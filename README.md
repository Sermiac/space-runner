## Space Runner

Space Runner is a pixel art platformer where you explore unknown planets, solve puzzles, and survive long enough to escape.

Each level takes place on a different planet with its own dangers, enemies, and environmental challenges. Your mission is to gather what you need, manage your oxygen, and find the exit to continue your journey.

## Gameplay

- Exploration of unique planets
- Levels designed as individual challenges
- Puzzles that unlock new paths
- Enemies to avoid or overcome
- A limited oxygen system that adds constant pressure
- Final objective in each level: return to the ship and escape

## Genre

2D platformer focused on exploration and light survival elements.

## Visual Style

Pixel art graphics with space and alien-themed environments.
